## ✨ Harry Potter Movie Animations & Interactive Features

1. **🏰 The Great Hall Hero Banner**:
   - High-resolution atmospheric Hogwarts castle night background with glowing windows and misty lake reflections.
   - 3D Floating Candles with real-time flickering flames, swaying at staggered heights.
   - Golden foil title typography with continuous shimmer animations.

2. **📜 The Hogwarts Acceptance Letter & Wax Seal**:
   - 3D crimson wax seal stamped with an owl and wand crest.
   - Clicking breaks the seal with sparks and opens the weathered parchment letter from Professor McGonagall.

3. **🪄 Interactive Wand Cursor & Spark Trailing**:
   - Interactive wand tip replacing the default mouse cursor.
   - Canvas particle engine emitting shimmering magical sparks that change colors depending on your chosen Hogwarts House.
   - Interactive mouse clicks trigger magical spell bursts.

4. **⚡ The Golden Snitch Mini-Game**:
   - Physics-driven Golden Snitch autonomously darting and banking across the screen with fluttering feathery wings.
   - Catch the snitch by clicking it to earn **+150 Points** with an explosion of golden particles and a victory toast.

5. **📰 The Daily Prophet Animated Newspaper**:
   - Moving sepia/monochrome wizard portrait with vintage television scanlines, light shifts, and tilting wizard hat.
   - Live headline: *"Brilliant MCA Scholar Arijith M Enrolls at CogniLearn!"*

6. **👣 The Marauder's Map Experience**:
   - *"I solemnly swear that I am up to no good"* interactive toggle button.
   - Animates footprint trails across the antique parchment connecting Arijith's journey milestones (Undergrad $\rightarrow$ MCA Studies $\rightarrow$ CogniLearn Internship $\rightarrow$ Full-Stack Sorcerer).
   - *"Mischief Managed"* button to fold the parchment back.

7. **🪄 Quick-Cast Spell Dock**:
   - **Lumos**: Darkens the screen and activates a flashlight beam following your wand cursor, uncovering hidden invisible runes!
   - **Wingardium Leviosa**: Causes the parchment cards and books to levitate with anti-gravity bobbing.
   - **Expecto Patronum**: Blasts a radiant silver wisp wave across the screen.
   - **Alohomora**: Unlocks the project vault chamber with golden gleams.
   - **Nox**: Extinguishes the Lumos beam.

8. **🎵 Web Audio API Sound Synthesizer**:
   - Built-in sound engine generating authentic celesta bells (Hedwig's theme motive), wand zaps, and spell chimes without external audio dependencies.
   - Sound toggle button with mute/unmute control.

9. **🦁 House Selector (Sorting Hat)**:
   - Switch between **Gryffindor**, **Slytherin**, **Ravenclaw**, and **Hufflepuff** to dynamically transform color palettes, mana bars, and magical spark effects.
